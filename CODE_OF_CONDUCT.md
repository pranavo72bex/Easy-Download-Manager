# Code of conduct

dm follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism
about the work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the
project is discussed.

To report behaviour that breaks this, email pranavj304@gmail.com. Reports
are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and
block people who keep breaking these rules.
