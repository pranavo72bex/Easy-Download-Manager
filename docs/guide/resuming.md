# Stop now, finish later

How to stop a download and have it go on later from where it was, step by
step.

1. Click the download in the list.
2. Click **Stop download** on its row (or the toolbar's **Stop**). The row
   says it is stopped; what it has fetched is kept.
3. When you want it to go on, click **Resume** on its row (or **Resume
   download** on its right-click menu). It goes on from the bytes it
   already has.

To know first whether a download can go on from where it stopped, open its
window (**Details** on its row) and read **Can resume**. **Yes** means it
can; "No — a stop starts it again from the beginning" means the server
does not allow it.

When you exit dm while downloads run, dm asks **Exit dm?** first: **Exit**
pauses them, and they pick up where they left off the next time you open dm.

A download that failed offers **Retry** instead; most go on from what they
already have. If its link has expired, give it a fresh one with
[Update download link](update-link.md).
