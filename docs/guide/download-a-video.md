# Download a video

How to save a video from the page it plays on, step by step.

1. Open the video's page in your browser, with dm's extension added (see
   [Downloads from your browser](browser-downloads.md)).
2. Play the video for a moment, then move the mouse over it. A small bar
   shows on its top-right corner: **Download video**.
3. Click **Download video**. Where the video comes in several qualities,
   its ▾ lists each first, tallest first, with its size, then the sound
   alone: click the one you want.
4. With **Options › General › Ask where to save videos** on,
   [Download details](download-details.md) opens: choose the folder and the
   name, and click **Start now**. Off, the video starts at once.

Without the browser:

1. Copy the video page's address.
2. Click **Add URL**, check the address is in **Address**, and click **OK**.
3. Download details says "Reading the video's qualities…", then shows each
   quality as a button with its size. Click one.
4. To keep only a part, tick **Only a part of the video** and give **from**
   and **to** (such as 4:10 and 4:45).
5. Click **Start now**.

A YouTube playlist or channel opens [Playlist](playlist.md) instead, with
one quality for every video. More on qualities: [Videos and their
qualities](videos.md).
