# Keep some speed for everything else

How to stop dm from using all of your connection, step by step.

1. Open the **Downloads** menu and point at **Speed limiter**.
2. Choose **Low speed (256 KB/s)**, **Medium speed (1 MB/s)** or **High
   speed (4 MB/s)**. The status bar says "Speed limiter on: … KB/s." It
   holds every running download together, torrents included, and takes
   effect within a moment.
3. For a speed of your own, choose **Speed limiter settings…**, type it in
   **Speed limit (KB/s, 0 for none)**, and click **OK**.
4. To go full speed again, choose **Full speed**. **Limit speed** turns the
   limit you had back on.

The same presets are on the status bar and on the tray icon's **Speed**
menu. A site or a single download can have a limit of its own; see
[Speed limiter](speed-limiter.md).
