# Move to queue

Move to queue puts the chosen downloads in another queue, or in a new one.
Open it with **File › Move to queue…** or **Move to queue** on the
right-click menu, with one or more downloads chosen.

## Fields and buttons

- The queues: one choice for each queue in the list. **Main queue** is
  picked at first.
- **Or a new queue:** type a name to make a new queue and move the
  downloads into it. While something is typed here, no queue above is
  picked; picking one empties the field.
- **Cancel:** closes the window and moves nothing.
- **OK:** moves every chosen download to the queue picked or typed.

## Keys

- **Return** in **Or a new queue** is **OK**.
- **Escape**, Ctrl+W (⌘W on macOS) and the close button are **Cancel**.

See [Scheduler](scheduler.md) for setting a queue's hours, and **Options ›
Downloads** for how many downloads each queue runs at once.
