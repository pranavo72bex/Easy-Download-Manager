# Statistics

**View › Statistics…** opens a window of figures about what EDM has
downloaded. They are worked out when the window opens, from the finished
downloads in the list: each one's size, when it finished, the site its
address is on and its kind. EDM writes nothing new to work them out, and
keeps nothing of them when the window closes. Asked for again while it is
open, it comes to the front.

## What it shows

- **Files finished** and **Downloaded in all:** how many downloads in the
  list finished, and their size together.
- **By day, the last 30 days:** a bar for each day, today at the right, as
  tall as that day's bytes against the busiest day's. The first and the
  last day are named under the bars, and "Busiest day: …, …" says which
  day it was and how much came that day.
- **By site, the 10 largest:** the sites the most bytes came from, the most
  first, each with a bar and its size. A download whose address names no
  site is counted under "no site".
- **By category:** the same for each kind (Videos, Audio, Documents, Apps,
  Archives, Pictures, Other), named as the side panel names them.
- **Speed:** **Average speed** is how fast the downloads went together, on
  average, while any of them ran since EDM opened; **Best speed** is the
  fastest they went together. Each says "not measured yet" until something
  has run. EDM measures these while it is open and forgets them when it
  closes.

With nothing finished yet it says "Nothing has finished yet."

A download removed from the list, or one marked **Keep no record**, is not
counted: the figures come from the list alone. **Close**, Escape or Return
closes the window.

## The speed line in a progress window

A download's progress window (see [Download progress](download-window.md))
shows, under its bar, its speed over the last five minutes: "Speed over the
last 5 minutes", a bar for each five seconds, as tall as it is against the
fastest, the latest at the right. It starts empty when the window opens and
is forgotten when it closes. It is not shown once the download has
finished, failed or been cancelled.
