# Downloads from your browser

How to have the downloads you start in your browser come to dm, step by
step.

1. Open **Options** (on macOS **dm › Settings…** or ⌘,; on Windows and Linux the toolbar's
   **Options** button) and choose **Browser**.
2. Under **Add dm to your browser**, click **Add** beside your browser. It
   opens the page where that browser adds dm's extension. Add it there.
3. Back in dm, click **Check now**. Your browser now says **Connected**.
4. Make sure **Take these file types from the browser** is on. **File
   types** lists the kinds dm takes, for example `zip exe mp4`; leave it
   empty for all.
5. Click **OK** to close Options.
6. In your browser, click a download link as usual. dm takes it instead of
   the browser.
7. With **Options › General › Ask where to save browser downloads** on,
   [Download details](download-details.md) opens first: choose the folder
   and click **Start now**. Off, the download starts at once.

To leave one download to the browser, hold **Alt** (or the key chosen under
**Hold while clicking to leave it with the browser**) while you click the link.
To send a link to dm yourself, right-click it in the browser and choose
**Download link with dm**.
