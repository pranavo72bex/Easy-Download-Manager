# The "Download video" panel

The browser extension shows a small panel over a video player so a person can
hand the video to dm. This page lists what the panel does and which test holds
each line. The panel carries dm's own mark and colours.

| #  | Behaviour                                                                                                   | Test |
|----|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------|
| 1  | Shown when playback starts and while the pointer is on the player, through any overlay; kept while the pointer rests | `chrome-check.py`, `youtube-check.py` |
| 2  | One compact bar on the player's top-right corner: dm's mark, "Download video", a ▾ when there is a choice, ? and × | `decide.rs` placement tests; `chrome-check.py` reads its place |
| 3  | A file `<video src>` is one quality: a click hands it over at once                                          | `chrome-check.py` |
| 4  | A player page (YouTube, any MediaSource player): the app asks its yt-dlp (`yt-dlp -J`) and the panel lists one line per height, tallest first, with the size, then the sound alone | `ytdlp.rs` `a_pages_formats_become_one_choice_per_height_and_the_sound_alone`; `host.rs`; `chrome-check.py` with a stand-in yt-dlp |
| 5  | A chosen line goes to the app with its quality and container, and the app opens "Download details" for it (name, folder, note, queue) before starting; Options › General can turn that off | `window.rs` |
| 6  | The app downloads it like any other row; the page can be closed                                             | the queue's own tests |
| 7  | × opens "Hide for now" (that player, until the page reloads) and "Never on <site>" (every page of the site, and its subdomains, until the toolbar popup turns it back on, which takes effect without a reload); ? says what the panel does | `decide.rs` site tests; `chrome-check.py` |
| 7a | Options › Browser in the app turns the panel off on every site, and back on, without a browser restart: the switch travels in the app's catch rules, which a page that finds a player asks to be current (the handshake is kept a minute) and every page's script watches | `dm-protocol` `the_rules_cross_as_written_and_read_from_either_spelling` |
| 8  | A site whose security policy forbids WebAssembly gets no panel; its right-click menu, Alt-click and "Download all links" still work (plain JavaScript in `content.js`, registered before the WebAssembly loads) | `chrome-check.py`; `decide.rs` `the_plain_page_script_answers_by_the_names_the_background_uses`; a CDP run on a `script-src 'none'` page (gap 48) |
| 9  | Videos on sites yt-dlp does not know: the HLS and DASH playlists and whole audio and video files the page's player loaded (seen in its network responses, never changed) are listed beside yt-dlp's qualities, or alone; segments and partial ranges are not | `decide.rs` stream tests; `chrome-check.py` stream pages; `firefox-check.py` |
| 10 | A player smaller than the panel (a 96px clip) gets none                                                     | `chrome-check.py` |
| 11 | Fullscreen: the panel goes inside the fullscreen element (only it is drawn) — into its open shadow root when it is a component — and back to the page's body after; a bare `<video>` or a closed component in fullscreen gets none | CDP run (gap 66) |
| 12 | Keyboard and screen readers: the focus on a player raises its panel and keeps it up; Tab goes from the player into the bar, ? and ×, and on through the page; Enter and Space press; the arrows, Home and End walk the list, which takes the focus when it opens; Escape closes the list, then hides the panel with the focus back on the player. The bar is named by what it says, and each result is read out (a polite live region) | `panel.rs` `keys_move_through_the_list_and_escape_backs_out`, `a_screen_reader_hears_what_the_bar_says_and_each_result`; CDP run with real key events (gap 66) |
| 13 | Not offered for a player no one sees: transparent or hidden (its own or an ancestor's opacity and visibility), or with something other than the player and its own layers at its middle — followed through shadow roots (a video inside a component under a control layer counts), and judged by the player's box (a letterboxed clip counts) | `panel.rs` `a_hidden_or_transparent_player_is_not_offered`, `a_player_is_hit_tested_where_it_is_on_screen`; CDP run (gap 66) |
| 14 | Drawn left to right on a right-to-left page; a long message wraps inside the panel | `panel.rs` `the_panel_is_drawn_left_to_right_and_long_messages_wrap`; CDP run on a `dir="rtl"` page (gap 66) |
